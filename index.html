Our First Valentine - 2021

I could not remember that our first Valentine's day was in Goa until our recent conversation with Sanad. While Valentine's Day might not have been special. I am glad we had a lovely Valentine's week and a start of lifetime valentines.

Before you, I had never known someone so carefree. You brought fun and excitement into my life in a way I had never experienced and you still continue to do so. Even though if you feel we have become boring couple, you still bring fun in my life which I would never ever in my lifetime could have experienced without you.

Our Long Distance Valentine - 2022

The Long distance was tough. But we ensured to make some moments for ourselves.

I find strength in your resilience. Any challenge that I faced and will face with you, I trust your strength and can rely on you and I hope you trust and can rely on me as well.

Our Valentine with our first pet - 2024

I remember the impromptu candle light dinner we had and fun photo sessions after that. It was the first valentine where we also included another creature in our family.

Your inherent kindness and compassion towards animals is astonishing, irritating and envious. I am proud to have in you a kind partner inside and out in the world of people who pretend to be kind.

A Valentine's Celebration turned even more celebratory! - 2025

The day felt even more special with your almost-there selection at Google — just one interview to go. Even if the date wasn’t ideal, the results of your hard work have truly came through.

Those were some of the days I truly enjoyed watching you work so sincerely toward your goal. More than attraction, what I felt was a deep sense of admiration.

2026 - Today making memories with you

Looking into your eyes, right now.

Being with you, in this moment, forming memories is extremely precious.

Your presence in my life is more precious than you can imagine. I enjoy sharing my mind, my space, my thoughts, my vulnerability and my time with you.
